### Josue Torres-Fonseca (he/him)
Hello! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in [Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan](https://cse.engin.umich.edu/), advised by [Dr. Joyce Chai](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~chaijy/) in the [SLED Lab](https://sled.eecs.umich.edu/). My research studies how embodied AI agents, from robots to multimodal language models, understand language and act safely in the physical world. Some directions I'm particularly interested in include:

- **Safety in embodied agents.** When multimodal agents recognize a household hazard, do they actually act to mitigate it, and where is that safety information lost between high-level planning and low-level control? (see [ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1852/), ongoing work on hierarchical and unified VLM-VLA systems)

- **Grounding language through interaction.** How can robots learn what words mean by interacting with the world around them, and how does grounding emerge inside language models? (see [ICML 2026](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=51914Feknq), [SIGdial 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.sigdial-1.14/), [SIGdial 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.sigdial-1.51/))

- **Conversational robots for well-being.** How can embodied conversational agents help people living with cognitive decline reflect on and strengthen their resilience, and how do people perceive a robot's behavior? (see [LREC 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lrec-1.617/), HRI 2027 under review, ongoing in-home study)

My work is supported by the [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/) and the Gemini Academic Program, and I've served as treasurer of Michigan's Computer Science and Engineering Graduate Students.
