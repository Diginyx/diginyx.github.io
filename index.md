### Josue Torres-Fonseca (he/him)
Hi! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by [Dr. Joyce Chai](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~chaijy/) in the [SLED Lab](https://sled.eecs.umich.edu/). I study how embodied agents understand language and act safely, from robots that learn language through interaction, to multimodal LLM agents that recognize and fix household hazards, to conversational robots that support people living with cognitive decline.

I'm supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and the Gemini Academic Program.
