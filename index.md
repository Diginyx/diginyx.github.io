### Josue Torres-Fonseca (he/him)
Hi! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Dr. Joyce Chai in the Situated Language and Embodied Dialogue (SLED) Lab. I study how embodied agents understand language and act safely. I'm currently tracing how safety information travels from vision-language planners to vision-language-action controllers, and extending ReMI, our conversational robot for people living with cognitive decline, into a multi-week in-home study. I'm supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and the Gemini Academic Program.
