### Josue Torres-Fonseca (he/him)
Hello! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in [Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan](https://cse.engin.umich.edu/), advised by [Dr. Joyce Chai](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~chaijy/) in the [SLED Lab](https://sled.eecs.umich.edu/). My research studies how embodied AI agents, from robots to multimodal language models, understand language and act safely in the physical world. Some directions I'm particularly interested in include:

- **Safety in embodied agents.** When multimodal agents recognize a household hazard, do they act to mitigate it, from high-level planning down to low-level control, and where inside these models is safety information lost? I study this with benchmarks and mechanistic interpretability. (see [ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1852/))

- **Grounding language.** How can robots learn what words mean by interacting with the world, and how does grounding emerge mechanistically inside language models? (see [ICML 2026](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=51914Feknq), [SIGdial 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.sigdial-1.14/), [SIGdial 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.sigdial-1.51/), [LREC 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lrec-1.617/))

- **Conversational robots for well-being.** How can embodied conversational agents help people living with cognitive decline reflect on and strengthen their resilience? (HRI 2027 under review)

My work is supported by the [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/). I enjoy working with and mentoring students, so if you're interested in any of these directions, feel free to reach out!
