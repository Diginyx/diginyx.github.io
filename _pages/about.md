Hi! I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, where I work with [Dr. Joyce Chai](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~chaijy/) as a member of the [Situated Language and Embodied Dialogue (SLED) Lab](https://sled.eecs.umich.edu/). My research focuses on language understanding, cognitive development, and embodied agents, specifically, how robots can take advantage of their ability to interact with the world around them to learn language during spoken interaction with others.

My recent work evaluates whether multimodal LLM agents can recognize and mitigate safety hazards while completing household tasks ([SafetyALFRED](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1852/), Findings of ACL 2026), and builds conversational robots that help people living with cognitive decline reflect on their resilience (under review at HRI 2027). I am currently studying how safety information travels from vision-language planners to vision-language-action controllers, using mechanistic interpretability to find where the safety signal lives and how to amplify it.

Prior to joining the University of Michigan, I graduated from Boise State University with a B.S. in Computer Science (Magna Cum Laude, Minor in Applied Mathematics), where during my last two years I performed research advised by [Dr. Casey Kennington](https://www.caseyreddkennington.com/) as a member of the Speech Language and Interactive Machines (SLIM) Lab. I am supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and the Gemini Academic Program.

# 💻 Research Experience

**PhD Candidate, Situated Language and Embodied Dialogue Group**, University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI &nbsp;·&nbsp; *2023.08 – Present*<br>
Supervised by Dr. Joyce Chai

- **Tracing the Safety Signal in VLM-Planner → VLA-Controller Pipelines** *(ongoing)*
  - Evaluating safety in hierarchical robot pipelines where a high-level VLM planner issues safety-aware instructions to a low-level VLA controller (π<sub>0.5</sub>) on long-horizon BEHAVIOR-1K household tasks, with hazards drawn from real injury data (NEISS)
  - Diagnosing whether unsafe outcomes originate in the planner or the controller
  - Using mechanistic interpretability (activation patching, SAEs, linear probing) to locate where the safety signal lives and how it travels from the VLM planner to the VLA action expert
  - Developing methods that amplify safety features identified with sparse autoencoders, preserving the safety signal during action prediction while maintaining task success; evaluated on held-out hazard categories
- **ReMI Longitudinal: Building Resilience through Multi-Week Robot Interaction** *(ongoing)*
  - Extending ReMI into a 2-week in-home deployment that aims to strengthen resilience in individuals living with cognitive decline through resilience education, guided discussion, and mindfulness practice
- **ReMI: A Conversational Robot to Elicit Resilience Reflection** *(HRI 2027, under review)*
  - Designed and built an embodied conversational robot that encourages individuals living with cognitive decline to reflect on their resilience, co-designed with 10 dementia experts and 14 people with cognitive impairment
  - Developed a structured dialogue manager that steps through rapport-building, challenges, and resilience reflection phases; blinded raters scored its pacing above end-to-end LLM prompting in 35 of 40 simulated conversations
  - Fine-tuned Qwen3-VL-32B on 15K dialogue turns distilled from Gemini 2.5 Pro, plus a resilience topic classifier reaching 84% accuracy; served both on-premise with vLLM to protect participant privacy
  - Deployed in 7 lab and 3 in-home studies with individuals living with cognitive decline
- **SafetyALFRED: Evaluating Safety-Conscious Planning of Multimodal LLMs** *(Findings of ACL 2026)*
  - Created a safety-centric dataset using PDDL to generate trajectories that complete household tasks while mitigating six categories of kitchen hazards across 30 AI2-THOR scenes
  - Evaluated 11 state-of-the-art VLMs on hazard recognition and safe embodied planning, finding models correctly recognized hazards up to 92% of the time but fixed them less than 60% of the time
  - Developed a decoupled multi-agent framework separating hazard recognition from task execution, raising the rate at which Qwen3-VL-32B fixed appliance-misuse hazards from 0.7% to 71.1%
  - Open-sourced the code and dataset; presented a poster at ACL 2026

**Undergraduate Researcher, Speech Language and Interactive Machines Group**, Boise State University, Boise, ID &nbsp;·&nbsp; *2021.08 – 2023.08*<br>
Supervised by Dr. Casey Kennington

- **Symbol and Communicative Grounding through Object Permanence on a Mobile Robot** *(SIGdial 2022)*
  - Designed and built an incremental spoken dialogue system capable of transcribing and understanding speech, detecting and gaining a concrete understanding of objects, and mapping objects in its environment using SLAM and the Anki Cozmo robot
  - Approximated object permanence by extending Cozmo's SLAM map to remember each object it has seen and its location; in an A/B study with 24 participants, this raised the robot's accuracy answering what/where questions about learned objects from 70.0% to 82.7% and nearly doubled the questions participants asked
- **HADREB: A Dataset of Human Appraisals of Robot Emotional Behaviors** *(LREC 2022)*
  - Collected and analyzed a dataset of over 1,000 robot emotional behaviors described and rated by 32 participants on the Misty II and Cozmo robots
  - Showed a zero-shot language model predicts perceived emotion from the descriptions with up to 79.3% accuracy
